# Sales-Profit-Executive-Dashboard
Imagine running a cookie company with steady sales, yet constantly worrying about three core questions: Which product is actually making money? Who are our most critical clients? And why do sales drop sharply in certain months?

That was the exact business challenge behind this dataset, and here is how I used Power BI to solve it step-by-step:

1. The Profitability Puzzle (Product Analysis)

The Problem: Revenue was coming in, but it was unclear which products drove real bottom-line profit.

The Finding: The Chocolate Chip cookie turned out to be the absolute powerhouse ($1.69M revenue / $1.01M net profit). Meanwhile, White Chocolate Macadamia Nut commanded the highest Average Order Value at $8,941.

2. The Client Risk (Customer Concentration)

The Problem: Sales looked healthy overall, but we needed to assess our reliance on major buyers.

The Finding: Over 54% of total company profits were generated by just two primary accounts (ACME Bites and Wholesome Foods). Identifying this concentration risk gives leadership a clear priority to retain these key relationships.

3. The Seasonal Rollercoaster (Timeline Trends)

The Problem: Unpredictable monthly fluctuations in revenue.

The Finding: Mapping the time series revealed a strong seasonal pattern with repeating quarterly peaks ($400K+) followed by cyclical dips. The takeaway: the product line is solid, but targeted promotions are needed during off-peak months to stabilize cash flow.

I led the full workflow—from raw data cleanup using Power Query and building a Star Schema model, to crafting custom DAX measures for financial KPIs and interactive visualizations.
